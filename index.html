Before you choose your BOOST pathway, let me quickly explain how this works.

BOOST has two pathways, but they both lead toward the same goal — employment.

The Rapid Employment Pathway is designed for people who are ready to move toward work more quickly. It focuses on practical employment readiness, financial stability, transferable skills, AI tools, and job-search support.

The Career Exploration and Development Pathway gives you more opportunity to explore and validate a career direction. You’ll look more closely at jobs, wages, career fit, transferable skills, industry options, and whether additional development or training is really needed.

One pathway isn’t better than the other. They simply respond to different needs. And this decision isn’t based on a single test or one answer.

Through your conversations with your Career Coach, the evidence may show that it makes sense to prioritize one pathway over the other. That decision is made individually, based on the skills and experience you already have, the career direction you’re considering, and how those things align with opportunities in the current labor market.

Your pathway can also change as you learn more. New information, new opportunities, or what you discover about yourself may point toward a different next step.

You don’t have to figure that out alone. Your Career Coach will help you review the evidence and determine which pathway gives you the strongest and most efficient route forward.

Whichever path you take, the goal remains the same: help you move toward meaningful, sustainable employment.

When you’re ready, let’s choose your BOOST pathway.

This is not a permanent lock-in. Your pathway can change as new evidence, opportunities, and what you learn about yourself point toward a different next step.
