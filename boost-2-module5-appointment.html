Getting ready for your appointment

Having a few items available can make your conversation easier. Your Career Coach will confirm which information is needed for your circumstances.

Have available: Photo IDGovernment-issued photo identification, if you have it.
Have available: Social Security cardStaff will need to view and verify your card and document that verification in a case note. The card will not be collected or copied.
If applicable: Address / ResidencyRecent mail, lease information, or a utility record showing your current address, if requested.
If applicable: Income / BenefitsRecent pay stubs, income records, or SNAP, TANF, or other benefit information, particularly if you report low income.
If applicable: Employment SituationUnemployment, layoff, or separation information related to your situation.
If applicable: Education / Veteran RecordsDiplomas, transcripts, certificates, licenses, training records, or DD-214 / veteran documentation when relevant.

Don't have everything? You can still meet with your Career Coach. Not every document applies to everyone, and staff can help identify accepted forms of verification or alternatives. Please don't upload sensitive documents through BOOST.
