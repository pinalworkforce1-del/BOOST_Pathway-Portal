Congratulations — you’ve completed the Career Exploration portion of BOOST.

Your progress has been saved and shared with our team. A member of the team will contact you to discuss next steps, including intake and enrollment.

This is a good stopping point for now.
Your Career Coach will help you decide what comes next after intake and enrollment. You do not need to complete the remaining BOOST activities before speaking with your Career Coach. Your progress is saved, so you can return and continue where you left off. If you choose to continue exploring now, nothing is locked.

While you wait, you can begin gathering documents

The exact documents needed depend on the program and your circumstances. Your Career Coach will confirm what applies to you. Having common documents available can help make intake easier.

IdentityGovernment-issued photo identification or other identity documentation you have available.
Address / ResidencyRecent mail, lease information, utility documentation, or another record showing your current address if requested.
Employment / IncomeRecent pay information, unemployment documentation, or other employment and income records that may apply.
Education / TrainingDiploma, transcript, certificates, licenses, or training records you already have.
Employment SituationLayoff, separation, unemployment, or employer documentation if it relates to your situation.
Other Eligibility DocumentsVeteran documentation or other records may be requested when relevant. Staff will tell you what is needed.

Do not delay meeting with staff because you do not have every document listed above. The team can help identify what is actually required and what alternatives may be accepted.
