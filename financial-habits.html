Rosie says

Before we get started, I want you to know something important. This isn’t a test, and there are no right or wrong answers. We’re not here to judge how you spend your money or tell you what your budget should look like. This quick financial stability check simply helps us understand what’s coming in, what’s going out, and whether there are expenses or barriers that could make it harder to reach your employment goals. What you share can help your career coach identify support services that may be available to help you move forward — things like transportation, work clothing, tools, childcare, or other employment-related needs. Participation in this financial check-in is voluntary. However, if you are requesting support services, it must be completed before those services can be issued. Just give us your best estimate. It doesn’t have to be perfect. The goal is simple: understand where things stand today, identify what might help, and keep you moving toward your employment goals.
